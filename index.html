From customer expectations to actionable improvements.

The Expectation Gap Method compares what a page promises with what buyers say they experienced.

01

Map the Promise

Examine publicly available product descriptions and identify the claims, features, and expectations communicated to buyers.

02

Examine the Evidence

Review publicly available customer feedback, record relevant source links, and group recurring themes without overstating the evidence.

03

Recommend the Fix

Turn supported findings into specific FAQ suggestions, clearer product descriptions, and prioritized improvement opportunities.

What a client could receive.

  • Competitor comparison
  • Review theme analysis
  • Evidence-linked findings
  • Product page improvement recommendations
  • FAQ draft suggestions
  • Prioritized action checklist

Reports reflect the evidence available. We do not claim every issue will be found, and recommendations do not guarantee better sales.

A simple four-step process.

  1. Define the product category and competitors.

  2. Examine publicly available product information and reviews.

  3. Organize evidence and identify potential gaps.

  4. Deliver a concise research report with actionable recommendations.
